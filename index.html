Level 1 · City

Start with a plot and $50,000.

Lay out roads, place buildings with proper setbacks, and watch the city come alive: cars, lights at night, smoke from chimneys.

  • Hover a building to inspect it
  • Day & night cycle

Level 2 · Building

Pinch into any building.

Each zoom level has its own feel: quick from city to building, slower and more careful as you go deeper. The camera remembers where you last were in every building.

  • Breadcrumb trail
  • Minimap pin
  • Pan across to neighbours

Level 3 · Floor

Lift the roof off a floor.

The floors above lift away so you can plan every hallway and room. Detail only loads for the building you're in, so it stays smooth on phones.

  • Double-tap to go one level deeper
  • Quieter, closer sound

Level 4 · Room

Then step inside and make it home.

Turn the camera all the way round, look up at the ceiling, and place every bed, rug and lamp yourself. This is the heart of ZoomCity.

  • Full 360° camera
  • Jump back to City in one tap
